# Feature: Progression and areas

- Choose an area at random for a run and choose a new area at random after each area boss. Areas may repeat; progression has no fixed final area.
- Show the current area, area number, encounter number, and a progress bar at the top of the battle screen. Progress represents the current encounter stage: encounter 1 of 10 shows 10%, and encounter 10 of 10 shows 100%.
- Show the matching area illustration behind the Pokémon in the battle arena, blurred and cover-fitted so it fills the available arena width and height.
- Each area has a PokeAPI type pool. Regular encounters are selected from Pokémon matching at least one of the area's favored types:
  - Forest: Grass, Bug, Fairy
  - Jungle: Grass, Bug, Poison
  - Cave: Rock, Ground, Dark
  - Volcano: Fire, Rock, Ground
  - Mountain: Rock, Flying, Ice
  - Desert: Ground, Rock, Fire
  - Beach: Water, Ground, Flying
  - Ocean / Seafloor: Water, Ice
  - River: Water, Grass
  - Swamp / Bog: Water, Poison, Grass
  - Snowfield: Ice, Water
  - Ruins: Psychic, Ghost, Rock
  - Graveyard: Ghost, Dark
  - City: Normal, Electric, Steel
  - Power Plant: Electric, Steel
  - Laboratory: Psychic, Poison, Steel
  - Fairy Grove: Fairy, Grass, Psychic
  - Dragon Cave: Dragon, Rock, Ground
  - Space / Outer Space: Psychic, Steel, Electric
  - Ancient Ruins: Ghost, Psychic, Steel
  - Dojo: Fighting, Psychic, Steel
- Each area has 10 encounter stages. The tenth is the area boss; the two future shop positions temporarily spawn regular Pokémon encounters.
- Opponent area starting levels start at 1 and increase by 5 when entering each new area. Within an area, regular opponents gain two levels spread across the nine regular encounter stages; the area's boss is one level above the last regular encounter. The next area's first opponent is two levels above the previous area's boss, keeping area starts five levels apart.
- Player Pokémon start at level 1 and gain five levels distributed across each area's 10 encounter stages. Set the party's level to the current stage's player level when advancing to an encounter, including after leaving the shop.
- When a party member's level increases, present its newly unlocked supported level-up moves before the next encounter; see `raid-battle.md` for move-learning and replacement rules.
- When a party member reaches an evolution level, ask whether to evolve in a popup. Declining keeps the evolution available to choose for free in the shop. Use level requirements from PokeAPI; evolutions requiring items, friendship, time, trades, or other special conditions become available at level 30.
- Select each opponent's move pool from supported level-up moves available at its encounter level; see `raid-battle.md` for pool size, selection, and persistence.
- Regular encounters and regular area-boss fallbacks exclude Legendary and Mythical Pokémon. An area boss has a 50% chance to be a type-matching Legendary or Mythical when an unused eligible candidate exists; otherwise, use a regular Pokémon. Area bosses have 25% increased Attack. The stat- and level-scaled grid formula applies to all opponents, with half the scaled board capacity used as HP blocks (rounded up). Regular capacities are calibrated to 1, 16, and 81 positions in areas 1, 7, and 10; area-boss capacities are 9, 25, and 144 at those milestones. All grids are capped at 12×12. A Legendary or Mythical area boss gets one additional row and column, subject to the cap.
- A legendary or mythical selected as an area boss is recorded immediately and cannot appear again later in the run, whether or not it is caught.
- Persist area identity, area number, encounter stage, opponent level and boss status, grid progress, and used special-boss IDs in SQLite.
- Keep area selection, encounter generation, progression, and boss tuning in shared game logic, not route components. Keep the tunable values isolated in the progression configuration.
- Provide battle-screen debug controls to defeat the current opponent, jump to the current area's boss, and revive and fully heal the entire party.
