# Feature: Starter Pokémon selection

- At the start of a new run, let the player choose one Pokémon from a selection of three.
- All three choices must be starter Pokémon, but they do not need to come from the same region.
- Pikachu and Eevee are eligible to appear as starter-selection choices, each with a chance to be included.
- Do not treat Pikachu or Eevee as guaranteed choices or replace the three-choice limit.
- Save the selected Pokémon as the initial party member for the new run.
- The exact selection pool, weighting, and chance for Pikachu and Eevee are not specified and must not be hard-coded as balance decisions without direction.
- Ensure the three choices share no types with one another. This applies to every type, including secondary types; for example, if Charmander is selected, no other Fire-type starter may appear.
- Let the player tap a choice to select it, then use a separate confirm button to save and begin the run.
- Show each choice's Pokémon name and types; Pokédex numbers are not required.
